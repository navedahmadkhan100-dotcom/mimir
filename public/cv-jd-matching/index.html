CV–JD MATCHING

Match a CV to a job description by requirement, evidence and capability.

Mimir compares a candidate CV with the actual job description rather than calculating a generic resume score. Each important requirement becomes a claim that must be supported by traceable candidate evidence.

What a useful CV–JD match should answer

A recruiter needs more than “82% match.” The useful questions are: Is this requirement explicitly demonstrated? Is the evidence recent enough? Did the candidate lead the work or only participate? Is a related technology an acceptable substitute? Does the role require an exact certification or merely equivalent knowledge?

Exact skills and transferable evidence

Mimir can distinguish exact matches from related capabilities. A related technology may deserve partial or strong credit where the hiring requirement allows substitution, while an explicitly mandatory tool can remain a hard boundary. The job description outranks generic assumptions.

Count, duration and ownership requirements

Requirements such as “three projects,” “five years,” “led a migration,” or “designed the architecture” should not be satisfied by a broad paragraph that merely sounds senior. Mimir's deterministic rules can require distinct qualifying projects, explicit duration evidence or ownership language before granting full credit.

Output built for recruiter review

The result combines an overall score with requirement-level evidence, claim assessments, Odin challenges, verification questions and score lineage. That lets a recruiter move quickly without losing the reasoning behind the recommendation.
