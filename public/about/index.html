ABOUT MIMIR

Mimir — Find the Worthy: evidence-based candidate intelligence built around recruiter judgement.

Mimir is a recruitment decision-support application created by Naved Khan, a Senior IT Recruiter. Its purpose is to make CV evaluation faster without reducing a candidate to an unexplained AI percentage.

Try Mimir CV EvaluatorSee how Mimir works

Why Mimir exists

Recruiters repeatedly need to answer the same hard question: does this CV actually prove the job requirement, or does it merely contain related language? Mimir was designed around that evidence gap.

The design philosophy

The language model is used for semantic interpretation, but the numerical score belongs to deterministic policy code. Evidence Graph, claim entailment, evidence boundaries and Odin are intended to make overclaiming easier to detect and recruiter review easier to audit.

Find the Worthy does not mean “let AI decide who deserves a job.” It means help the recruiter find the candidate whose evidence best establishes the role requirements, while keeping uncertainty visible.
