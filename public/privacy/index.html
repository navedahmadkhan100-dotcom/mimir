PRIVACY & DATA HANDLING

What happens to a CV when you use Mimir.

Mimir is designed to minimise unnecessary candidate identity data while preserving the professional evidence needed for recruitment evaluation. This page describes the current technical behaviour of the live application; it is not a substitute for an employer’s privacy notice or legal assessment.

Current document flow

BROWSER

Prepare the document

PDF, DOCX or pasted CV content is processed client-side to extract text and substantive visuals.

MINIMISE

Reduce direct identifiers

Basic candidate contact/header PII is redacted locally where detected. Text is remasked server-side as defence in depth.

EVALUATE

Assess professional evidence

Prepared evidence is evaluated against the structured JD. The current deployment does not use a server database for raw CV storage.

Visual CV evidence

Technical diagrams, architecture drawings and charts can be professionally relevant evidence. In practical privacy mode, Mimir preserves those visuals for evaluation rather than blocking the CV because an ambiguous visual may contain PII. Users should avoid uploading unnecessary personal photos or identity documents.

Local persistence

Saved job-description structures and Evidence Intelligence observations are browser-local in the current build. Clearing browser storage removes those locally stored items. Evaluation metadata may be returned to the browser as part of the result.

Important limitation

No automated PII detector is perfect. Mimir reduces obvious direct identifiers but should not be treated as a complete anonymisation service. Organisations using candidate data remain responsible for their own privacy, security, retention and legal obligations.
