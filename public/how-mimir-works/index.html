MIMIR ARCHITECTURE

How Mimir turns a CV and JD into an evidence-backed recruiter decision.

Mimir is built around a simple separation of responsibilities: the model interprets semantic evidence, deterministic code controls scoring, an adversarial verifier challenges overclaiming, and the recruiter remains responsible for the final decision.

Try Mimir CV EvaluatorSee how Mimir works

1. Document intelligence

PDF and DOCX files are prepared in the browser. Mimir extracts text and detects substantive visual artefacts such as architecture diagrams, technical drawings, topology views and decision flows. Basic direct candidate identifiers are reduced locally before prepared evidence is submitted for evaluation.

2. Freeze the job description

On the first candidate for a new job description, Mimir structures the JD before the candidate CV is shown to the model. This isolates role definition from candidate-controlled content and creates a reusable structured requirement set.

3. Build claims and evidence

Requirements become claims. CV quotes and approved visuals become evidence. Mimir records action, ownership, scope and other evidence semantics so a mention is not automatically treated as proof of delivery.

4. Entailment and evidence boundaries

The entailment layer asks whether the evidence actually establishes the claim. Evidence boundaries prevent weak evidence from being upgraded into a stronger conclusion—for example, supporting a system does not automatically prove that the candidate designed its architecture.

5. Odin adversarial verification

Odin challenges conclusions that stretch beyond the evidence and can downgrade them before scoring. Remaining uncertainty is converted into focused recruiter questions instead of being hidden.

6. Deterministic scoring and score lineage

The AI model does not choose the numerical candidate score. Mimir's JavaScript scoring policy converts validated evidence states into points and records the lineage of the result. This creates a clearer separation between probabilistic interpretation and numerical decision logic.

Mimir's operating rule: AI interprets. Mimir verifies. JavaScript scores. Humans decide.
