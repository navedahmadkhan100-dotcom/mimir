Mimir / AI CV Screening
EXPLAINABLE RECRUITMENT SCREENING

AI CV screening should show the recruiter what the candidate actually proved.

Mimir turns CV screening into an evidence review. Instead of treating every keyword as equal, it evaluates how strongly the candidate evidence supports each requirement and keeps unsupported or ambiguous claims visible for recruiter verification.

From keyword screening to evidence screening

A CV can contain the right words without proving the right capability. A candidate may list Kubernetes without showing production ownership, mention architecture without demonstrating design responsibility, or work around a technology without using the exact mandatory tool. Mimir is designed to preserve those distinctions.

How the screening pipeline works

ROLE

Freeze the JD

Requirements are structured before the candidate is shown to the model, reducing candidate-controlled prompt influence.

EVIDENCE

Assess claims

Evidence semantics, entailment and boundaries determine what each quote or visual is allowed to establish.

REVIEW

Challenge uncertainty

Odin attacks over-reaching interpretations and creates targeted verification questions.

Why explainability matters in recruitment

A recruiter should be able to answer: Which requirement gained these points? Which CV evidence supports it? Was the evidence direct, partial, contextual or missing? What still needs to be checked at interview? Mimir exposes those layers rather than hiding them behind one score.

Human-controlled by design

Mimir should not be used as an automatic rejection engine. Scores and evidence are intended to support professional recruiter judgement, especially when requirements are ambiguous or candidate evidence is incomplete.
